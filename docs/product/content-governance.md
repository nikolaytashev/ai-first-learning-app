# Learning-Content Governance

No lesson may be published solely because an agent generated or edited it.

## Required lifecycle

1. Record the proposed topic, audience level and learning objectives.
2. Record every source and its licence or permitted use.
3. Draft the lesson and quiz without copying protected source text.
4. Perform technical review for correctness and currency.
5. Perform editorial review for clarity, accessibility and alignment with the
   learning objectives.
6. Obtain a human publication approval.
7. Store a content version and review date.
8. Re-review content when a dependency or technical claim materially changes.

## Required metadata

- Stable content identifier and version.
- Author or generating-agent provenance.
- Source references and licence status.
- Technical reviewer and review date.
- Publication approver and approval date.
- Intended audience and prerequisites.
- Learning objectives.
- Next review date or review trigger.

## Decisions required

- Approved source and licence policy.
- Named human reviewers and required qualifications.
- Review cadence for time-sensitive AI material.
- Correction, withdrawal and user-notification process.
- Whether users may submit content or corrections.

Until these decisions are approved, agents may draft content proposals but may
not mark learning content as publishable.
